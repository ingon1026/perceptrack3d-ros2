  One way to express this is  Q^T Q = Q Q^T = I,
  where Q^T is the transpose of Q and I is the identity matrix.
  This leads to the equivalent characterization: a matrix Q is orthogonal
  if its transpose is equal to its inverse:  Q^T = Q^{-1},
  where Q^{-1} is the inverse of Q.
